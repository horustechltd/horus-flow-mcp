# Horus Flow & Cortex Intelligence

## Tagline
Cognitive market brain for autonomous AI trading agents (Orderflow physics & Contradiction Engine)

## Description
Horus Flow & Cortex Intelligence is an institutional-grade market intelligence engine engineered specifically for autonomous AI trading agents (Claude Desktop, Cursor, Cline, AutoGen) and quantitative execution bots.

Instead of confusing LLMs with noisy, lagging candlestick indicators (RSI, MACD), Horus measures live market gravity:
- Sub-second Limit Orderbook (L2) imbalance and aggressive taker volume deltas.
- Real-time liquidation cascade maps that pinpoint gravity magnets and forced squeeze zones.
- Cross-exchange speculation indexes that expose when markets are over-leveraged and vulnerable to flash crashes.
- A Level 4 Cognitive Cortex that fuses 7 independent evidence families with a real-time Contradiction Engine, providing penalized trust scores, strict machine-executable position sizing directives, and concrete USD invalidation boundaries.
